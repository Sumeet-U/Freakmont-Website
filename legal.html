What this is

Freakmont HQ is a fan-made statistics tracker for the Freakmont guild (Bera 5) in MapleStory Idle. It displays Combat Power, Guild War, Guild Conquest, Guild Boss Battle, Training Ground, and World Boss standings pulled from publicly viewable in-game leaderboard pages. It's built and maintained by guild members, for guild members — not a commercial product, and not run by Nexon.

Data & Privacy

This site collects nothing about you directly — no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no cookies, and nothing saved in your browser's local storage. It's static pages that read pre-built data files; there's no server-side code and no database.

The character stats shown (Combat Power, scores, rankings, etc.) are the same information already visible to anyone who visits the public in-game guild/character pages this site reads from — nothing here is private or exclusive to this site.

Data sources & credits

Guild and character data is pulled from mapleidle.gg (primary) and msidle.gg (secondary info), both community-run MapleStory Idle statistics sites. Charts on player pages are rendered with Chart.js, an open-source library.

Contact

Questions, comments, or corrections — reach out in the Freakmont Discord, or ping kbaFM directly in-game.
